## 1. Flipkart Customer Service Analysis

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, Data Analysis

Analysis of 30,000 customer service interactions to evaluate customer satisfaction, sentiment, support-channel performance and SLA compliance.

**Key Highlights:**
- Tested five business hypotheses.
- Identified billing questions as 72% of support interactions.
- Analysed customer satisfaction across four support channels.
- Developed business recommendations to improve customer experience and retention.

[View Project & Dashboard](flipkart-customer-service-analysis/)

## 3. Amazon Brazil Market Analysis

**Tools:** PostgreSQL, SQL, Excel

**Key Highlights:**
- Analysed customer purchasing behaviour and payment preferences.
- Studied seasonal revenue and product category performance.
- Performed customer segmentation and high-value customer analysis.
- Demonstrated joins, subqueries, CTEs, recursive CTEs and window functions.
